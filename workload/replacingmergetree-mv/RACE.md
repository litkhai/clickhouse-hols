# Partition rebuild racing the MV

Generated by `race.py --report-only` from `results/race-*.json`.

A writer inserts 1,000 new active-day orders every 0.05 s (a target; `writes` = how many it managed) for 60 s (amount 1 each), and during the first half also 1,000 late corrections of closed-day orders (amount 1 → 3, higher version). A rebuilder alternates `INSERT INTO stage SELECT … FROM orders FINAL WHERE day = d` + `REPLACE PARTITION d FROM stage` between the two days, 40 times planned. Then the writer stops; then one rebuild of each day with no writer (control). `diff` = target amount − source `FINAL` amount.
